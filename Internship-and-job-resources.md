# 🚨 Internship Scam Alert: Read Before You Apply

Protect your career, money, and personal information. Fake internship offers, recruitment scams, and unfair employment agreements can target students and freshers.

## 🔴 1. Never Pay to Get an Internship

Be cautious if someone demands:
- Registration or selection fees.
- Security deposits before joining.
- Money to release an offer letter, stipend, or certificate.
- Payments to a personal UPI ID for guaranteed placement.

**Remember:** Never pay a recruiter simply to secure a job or internship. Verify any paid training programme carefully before enrolling.

## 🟠 2. Identify Fake Offers

Watch out for:
- Unrealistically high stipends or guaranteed jobs.
- Instant selection without a meaningful process, especially when payment is demanded.
- Fake offer letters and suspicious email addresses.
- Pressure to accept offers immediately.
- Recruiters who cannot verify their identity or company.

**Safety tip:** Verify the opportunity through the company's official website before accepting.

## 🟡 3. Read Employment Bonds Carefully

Not every employment bond is illegal, but unclear or unfair terms can create problems.

Before signing:
- Read the complete agreement.
- Understand the bond amount, duration, and exit conditions.
- Never sign blank documents or cheques.
- Request a signed copy and seek legal advice if necessary.

## 🔐 4. Protect Your Personal Information

- Never share OTPs, passwords, UPI PINs, or card PINs.
- Avoid sending sensitive identity documents to unverified recruiters.
- Never install unknown applications or provide remote access at a recruiter's request.

## 🛡️ 5. Report Suspected Scams

If you encounter a suspicious offer:
1. Stop communicating or paying until you verify it.
2. Save screenshots, messages, offer letters, and payment records.
3. Report the listing to the relevant platform.
4. If you suspect financial cyber fraud in India, contact your bank and report it promptly.

**Official resources:**
- [National Cyber Crime Portal](https://cybercrime.gov.in/)
- **Cyber Fraud Helpline:** 1930
- [National Consumer Helpline](https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/)

## ✅ Before Accepting an Internship

- Verify the company and recruiter.
- Check the job description and stipend.
- Read all agreement terms.
- Confirm whether any fees are involved.
- Never let urgency or fear pressure you into paying.

---

### 🚫 Final Reminder

**Verify first. Never pay a recruiter to secure an internship. Never sign an agreement you don't understand.**

*This guide provides general awareness and is not legal advice.*
