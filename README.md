Internship & Job Resources for Students 🚀
A student-friendly directory of websites to find internships, fresher jobs, remote opportunities, technical roles, government internships, and career programs — with an emphasis on opportunities accessible to students in India.
Important: This repository lists discovery platforms, not guaranteed openings. Availability, eligibility, deadlines, and stipend vary by listing. Always verify the details on the employer's official website before applying.

Quick Start
1. Pick a category below.
2. Open a platform and search with your role, skills, location, and experience level.
3. Filter for Internship, Fresher, Entry Level, Remote, or Graduate where relevant.
4. Check the company, job description, eligibility, stipend/salary, deadline, and application URL.
5. Never pay a recruiter to secure a job or internship. Be cautious with requests for sensitive documents or financial information.

✅ Tips for Students
1. Use more than one source. No single platform lists every opportunity.
2. Customize your resume. Match relevant skills and projects to the job description.
3. Use specific search terms. Add your role, location, experience level, and intern or fresher.
4. Apply early. Some internships close as soon as positions are filled.
5. Verify before applying. Prefer the employer's official careers page when possible.
6. Watch for scams. Do not pay a recruiter for an offer, interview, or guaranteed placement. Be careful with suspicious training fees and requests for banking details or OTPs.
7. Track applications. Record the company, role, date applied, deadline, and status in a spreadsheet.
8. Check eligibility. Some programs have degree, graduation-year, location, or work-authorization requirements.
