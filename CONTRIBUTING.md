# Contributing

Thanks for helping students discover internships and jobs!

## Before adding a website

- Confirm the site is relevant to internships, graduate jobs, student programs, or career development.
- Use the official website URL where possible.
- Check that the link opens and does not redirect to suspicious pages.
- Describe the platform honestly. Do not claim that all listings are verified unless that can be demonstrated.
- Do not add personal data, referral spam, expired individual vacancies, or paid-placement schemes with unclear terms.

## How to contribute

1. Fork this repository.
2. Edit `README.md` and add the site under the most relevant heading.
3. Add a matching row to `lists/websites.csv`.
4. Open a pull request explaining what you added and why it is useful.

## Suggested CSV format

`category,website,best_for,url,notes`

Please keep entries concise and avoid duplicates.
